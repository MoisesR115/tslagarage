# TSLAgarage

Pick an engine, start it, and drive. TSLAgarage makes a silent EV (or any browser) sound like the car of your choice, with every sound synthesized live. Nothing is a recording.

**Vehicles:** Hellcat V8, Flat-Six GT, V12 Gran Turismo, Twin-Turbo V6, Haunted Hearse, Starship Whine.

**Drive modes:** Pedal (on-screen pedals or arrow keys), Cruise, and GPS, which follows your real speed.

**Gearbox:** Automatic by default. Switch to Manual and the gears move only when you move them. Scroll up to shift up, scroll down to shift down, and press and hold the wheel to rev. In a Tesla browser, the left scroll wheel does all of this. On touch devices, on-screen shift buttons appear in Manual. A red up arrow appears beside the gear number at the redline, an upshift flashes yellow, and a downshift shows a yellow down arrow.

**Settings:** Light, Dark, or System display.

Built with JavaScript and the Web Audio API. No audio files, no backend, one self-contained page.
